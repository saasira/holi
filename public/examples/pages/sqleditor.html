SQL editor

Keywords, strings, numbers, comments and :name parameters are coloured. Type two letters for suggestions (or Ctrl+Space); Tab or Enter accepts. Ctrl+Enter runs the selection, or all of it; Ctrl+/ comments lines. Each parameter gets a row for its type and value.

-- Revenue by region for a period SELECT region, count(*) AS orders, sum(total) AS revenue FROM orders WHERE placed_at >= :from AND region <> 'n/a' AND total > 10.5 GROUP BY region ORDER BY revenue DESC LIMIT :top;
